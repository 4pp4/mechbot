UNIT 1: THERMODYNAMICS - CARNOT CYCLE
The Carnot cycle is a reversible cycle made of four processes: isothermal heat addition at the
source temperature T_H, reversible adiabatic (isentropic) expansion, isothermal heat rejection at
the sink temperature T_L, and reversible adiabatic compression. Its efficiency depends only on the
two reservoir temperatures: eta = 1 - T_L / T_H, with temperatures in kelvin. Example: a Carnot
engine working between 527 C (800 K) and 27 C (300 K) has efficiency 1 - 300/800 = 62.5 percent.
No real engine operating between the same two temperatures can be more efficient (Carnot theorem).
Common mistake: using degrees Celsius instead of kelvin in the efficiency formula.

UNIT 2: STRENGTH OF MATERIALS - STRESS AND STRAIN
Normal stress sigma = P / A, measured in pascal (N/m^2), usually quoted in MPa. Normal strain
epsilon = change in length / original length and is dimensionless. Within the elastic limit Hooke's
law holds: sigma = E * epsilon, where E is Young's modulus (about 200 GPa for structural steel and
70 GPa for aluminium). Poisson's ratio nu = - lateral strain / longitudinal strain, about 0.3 for
steel. Factor of safety = yield (or ultimate) stress / working stress. For a simply supported beam
with a central point load W over span L, the maximum bending moment is W L / 4 at mid-span, and the
bending stress is given by the flexure formula sigma = M y / I.

UNIT 3: CNC MACHINING BASICS
A CNC part program is written in G-codes (preparatory functions) and M-codes (miscellaneous
functions). G00 is rapid positioning and must never be used to move into the material. G01 is
linear interpolation at the programmed feed rate F (mm/min). G02 is clockwise circular
interpolation and G03 counter-clockwise; the arc centre is given by I and J, which are incremental
distances from the arc start point to the centre, or by the radius R. G21 selects metric units and
G90 absolute programming, while G91 selects incremental programming. M03 starts the spindle
clockwise at speed S (rpm), M05 stops it, M06 performs a tool change, M08 turns coolant on and M09
turns it off. M30 ends the program and rewinds. A safe program always retracts the tool to a
clearance height in Z before making rapid moves in X and Y.
Cutting speed V = pi * D * N / 1000 (m/min), where D is the cutter diameter in mm and N the spindle
speed in rpm. Feed rate F = f_z * z * N, where f_z is feed per tooth and z the number of teeth.
